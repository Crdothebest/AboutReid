# 论文摘要

## 中文摘要

跨模态行人重识别是计算机视觉领域的重要研究方向，旨在解决不同成像模态（如RGB、近红外、热红外）之间的行人匹配问题。现有方法在处理多模态数据时存在尺度信息丢失、特征融合不充分等问题。本文提出了一种基于多尺度Mixture-of-Experts（MoE）的特征融合机制，用于提升跨模态行人重识别任务的性能。

本文的主要贡献包括：（1）提出了多尺度滑动窗口特征提取机制，通过4×4、8×8、16×16三种不同尺度的滑动窗口捕获局部细节、中等结构和全局上下文信息；（2）设计了专家网络专业化处理机制，为每个尺度分配专门的专家网络，实现专业化分工；（3）提出了动态门控融合策略，根据输入内容自适应调整各专家的权重，实现智能特征融合；（4）将多尺度MoE机制与CLIP预训练模型和Mamba状态空间模型集成，构建了完整的跨模态行人重识别框架。

实验在RGBNT201数据集上进行，结果表明所提出的方法相比基线方法在mAP和Rank-1指标上分别提升了2.6%和2.2%，验证了多尺度MoE机制的有效性。消融实验进一步证明了各组件对性能提升的贡献。此外，专家使用情况分析表明不同尺度专家能够有效分工，门控机制能够根据输入内容动态调整权重分布。

**关键词：** 跨模态行人重识别；多尺度特征；Mixture-of-Experts；专家网络；动态门控；CLIP

## 英文摘要

Cross-modal person re-identification is an important research direction in computer vision, aiming to solve the person matching problem between different imaging modalities (such as RGB, near-infrared, and thermal infrared). Existing methods suffer from scale information loss and insufficient feature fusion when processing multi-modal data. This paper proposes a multi-scale Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) feature fusion mechanism to improve the performance of cross-modal person re-identification tasks.

The main contributions of this paper include: (1) A multi-scale sliding window feature extraction mechanism that captures local details, medium-scale structure, and global context information through 4×4, 8×8, and 16×16 sliding windows; (2) An expert network specialization mechanism that assigns dedicated expert networks for each scale to achieve specialized processing; (3) A dynamic gating fusion strategy that adaptively adjusts expert weights based on input content for intelligent feature fusion; (4) Integration of the multi-scale MoE mechanism with CLIP pre-trained models and Mamba state-space models to construct a complete cross-modal person re-identification framework.

Experiments conducted on the RGBNT201 dataset show that the proposed method achieves improvements of 2.6% and 2.2% in mAP and Rank-1 metrics compared to baseline methods, validating the effectiveness of the multi-scale MoE mechanism. Ablation experiments further demonstrate the contribution of each component to performance improvement. Additionally, expert usage analysis shows that different scale experts can effectively divide labor, and the gating mechanism can dynamically adjust weight distributions based on input content.

**Keywords:** Cross-modal person re-identification; Multi-scale features; Mixture-of-Experts; Expert networks; Dynamic gating; CLIP
